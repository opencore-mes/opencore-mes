A report layout says which blocks an AI report has, in which order and how wide, and what each is for. It holds no query: whoever asks the analytics copilot for a report picks the layout, and the copilot fills each block from what that person may read. On a live design, or a change you may not edit, every control here is read only.

Its tabs

The tabs
General opens first. Click a tab to see its settings; the open one is highlighted.
A number on a tab
How many things on that tab differ from the live version. On Changes, the total.
Remove, and Undo
Every remove asks first. What is removed leaves this change only; nothing live changes until the change executes, and Undo (⌘Z or Ctrl+Z) brings it back.

Copilot

The conversation
Ask the AI to draft, check or explain the layout. It works as you, saves into this draft, and never submits. The conversation is kept with the change: it picks up here any time.
Ask the copilot…
Type what you want. ⌘ Enter or Ctrl+Enter sends; you can paste or drop a file into the box.
Attach
Adds a picture, a PDF, a CSV file or an Excel workbook (up to 5); × on a file takes it off.
Send
Saves your unsaved edits first, then sends. Greyed out while it works, or when no AI is set up.
New conversation
Asks first, then deletes the conversation kept with this change. The draft stays as it is.

Changes

The differences
What this change does to the layout against the live version, grouped by tab, block by block: how many changed, were added and removed. Reviewers and approvers see the same.
Open …
Jumps to the tab where that group is edited.
A difference
Marked new, changed or removed; click it to open or close it. Lines marked − are the live version's, lines marked + the draft's.

General

Name
Fixed once created.
Label
A text field: what the person asking for a report picks it by.
Description
A text box: what the layout is for, shown under it when it is picked.
Type
A drop-down: A report on the plant, or An AI assisted line, about the equipment you pick here: the copilot says which lots to process, and in which order, to meet the goal. For another line, copy the layout from the Report layouts tab and pick that line's equipment.
Records of
For a line: a drop-down of the object whose records make up the line (its equipment). Changing it clears the records picked.
Which
The records picked, as chips, at most 50, each by what people call it. Type in the box to find them, then click + name to add one (the list stays, for the next); or type a name in full and press Enter. × on a chip removes it.
The goal
A text box: what is to be achieved with those records, and where the targets are found if they are data. Required for a line.
Guidance for the copilot
A text box: words the copilot is given about the whole report, such as the period it covers, what to lead with and how to name things.

Blocks

A block
One card per block, in reading order: at least one, at most 12. A report drawn on the layout has exactly these blocks. A card new or changed in this change is marked at its left edge.
Its number and kind
Where it comes in the report, and what it is.
Earlier, Later
The arrows move it up or down one place.
copy
Puts a copy just below it: for an AI assisted line, the same block for another set of records.
remove
Takes the block out, after asking. Not shown on the last one left.
Kind
A drop-down: text (the copilot's words), a figure (one number), a chart, a table, or an AI assisted line (advice on a set of records).
Width
A drop-down: a quarter of the page, a third, half, or the whole row. A narrow screen gives it more.
Chart
For a chart: the copilot chooses the kind that answers the question, or pick one (bar, line, pie, Pareto…) to fix it. Its hint says what that kind is for.
Title
A text field: kept as written on every report. Empty, the copilot titles it.
Records of, Which
For an AI assisted line block: the object and its records the block is about, picked as on General.
The goal
For an AI assisted line block: what is to be achieved with its records.
What belongs here
A text box, said to the copilot and shown to the person picking the layout: what, over which period, in which order. The copilot writes the query.
+ add a block
A drop-down: pick a kind to add a block of it at the end, at that kind's usual width.

Preview

The page
How a report drawn on it is laid out: each block where it will be and as wide, with its number, kind, title, records, goal and what belongs there. Its content is the copilot's.
A place
One block's place. Change it on Blocks.

Stewards

Departments
Tick each department that approves every change to it. At least one is needed. A layout reads and writes nothing: a report drawn on it is read by each person with their own rights.

JSON

The layout as JSON
All of it as data. Edit it here, then apply it.
Apply JSON
Replaces the draft with it; every check runs as on any other edit. Its name must stay the same; what is wrong is said in red beside the change.
