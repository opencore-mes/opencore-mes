The sandbox runs this change's draft on copies of real records, in a database of its own: nothing in the live database is written. You pick the records it starts with, run steps on them as any person, and keep what you ran as a scenario: the evidence the fitness test runs again whenever the change is submitted.

The sandbox of this change
Back to the change returns to its design. A sandbox closes by itself after a quarter of an hour unused.

1. The records it starts with

The list
Each record the sandbox starts with, by its key (@lot): picked from live, or given here; and, once open, what it is in the sandbox now. Records they refer to come with them.
Key
A text field: the short name later steps use for it, written @key.
Object
A drop-down: the kind of record (a lot, a machine).
How
A drop-down: picked from live (a copy of a real record) or given here (made from the data you write).
Record
Picked from live: scan or type its label, then press Enter.
Where
Or say where to find one, such as {"state": ["waiting"]}: if the record picked is gone or has moved on when a scenario runs again, the newest that matches is used.
Its data, State
Given here: its fields as JSON ("@key" names another record of the sandbox), and the state it starts in.
Add
Puts it on the list.
Open the sandbox
Makes the sandbox with these records (copies of what you may see; records you may not see are not copied). Start again makes fresh copies; Close drops it.

2. Run a step

What
A drop-down: run a transaction, take a record's action, edit a record, make one, call a service, open a screen, or answer a plan.
Answer a plan
The plan waiting on a record: fill its input screen in (Values, as JSON), pick a manual decision's Choice, acknowledge or retry its wait, or let the wait's time pass (only here).
Which, Record, Action
Drop-downs: which transaction (those of this change marked so), which record of the sandbox, which of its actions.
As
A drop-down of people: the step runs as them, their roles deciding as they will live.
Its inputs
The transaction's own fields: drop-downs of the sandbox's records, text and number fields, a checkbox for yes or no, a table's rows as JSON.
Signed
A checkbox: tick it to sign, for a transaction that asks for a signature.
Data, Input, Name it
An edit's or a new record's fields, or a service's input, as JSON; a record made can be named for the steps after it.
Run
Runs it in the sandbox, as the person chosen.
A scenario's steps
Opened from a scenario: its steps, to run again in one go.

3. What ran

Ran
A step that ran: the states it left, the records it made.
Where its runs are
The route step or plan node each record has reached after the step.
Refused
A step that was refused, in the words the person would see. A scenario may expect that too.
Keep it as a scenario of, Named
A drop-down of this change's transactions and flow templates, and a name saying what it shows. A template's scenario keeps the node each record reached.
Save the scenario
Keeps the records and steps, with what each did, in the transaction's draft. It goes with every version of it, and the fitness test runs it again.
