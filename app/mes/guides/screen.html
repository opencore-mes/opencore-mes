A screen is a page put together from blocks: figures, lists, charts and forms. Everything on it is live: it changes as soon as anyone records something.

Its name
The screen, and the record it was opened with (a machine, a tool), if any.
Opened with
Pick or scan the record the screen is about; each has its own address and tab.
Maximize
Fills the window: for a tablet at a machine, or a board on a wall.
Tabs
Some blocks are grouped under tabs: pick one to see its blocks.
Figures
A count or a total, as of now.
Tables
The records it lists. Click a row's name to open it; a row's buttons run a transaction on it, the record filled in for you.
Charts
How the records divide, by a field.
Forms
A transaction, right on the screen: fill it in, check it, confirm it.
