A flow template is drawn here: a route a traveler (a lot) goes along step by step, a plan (an OCAP) an event sets off, or an input flow that fills a transaction or a screen from the keyboard. It is approved like every other design.

Its tabs

The tabs
Copilot, Changes, Flow (the canvas), General, Context, Participants, Scenarios, Stewards and JSON. An input flow has no Context, Participants or Scenarios.
… more
When the tabs do not all fit, the last ones are under this button: click it and pick one.
The number on a tab
How many things on that tab differ from the live version. The number on Changes counts them all.
This window shows
When the change holds more than this template, pick here what this window shows.
Undo, Redo
Above the windows: take back the last edit, or bring it again (⌘Z or Ctrl+Z, ⇧⌘Z or Ctrl+Y, outside a text box). Moving a node is an edit too.
Remove
Removing a node, a wire, a field, a value, a record or a scenario asks you to confirm first; Undo brings it back.

Copilot

Copilot
Ask the AI to draft, check or explain the template. It works as you, through the design tools, and never submits. It works inside a change only.
The conversation
What you asked, what it answered, and each tool it used (ticked, or crossed when it failed). It is kept with the change and picks up where it stopped.
Ask the copilot…
Write what you want; ⌘ or Ctrl and Enter sends it. Paste or drop a picture, a PDF, a CSV or an Excel file into the box to give it one.
Attach
Adds up to 5 files to your next message; × on a file takes it off.
Send
Sends your message. While it works, it shows the step it is on.
New conversation
Deletes the conversation kept with this change, after you confirm; the draft stays as it is.

Changes

Changes
Everything that differs from the live version: how many changed, added and removed, grouped by tab.
Open Flow, Open General…
Opens that tab in this window.
Each difference
Click it to fold or unfold it: lines taken out are marked −, lines added +.

Flow: drawing

Add (the palette)
Adds a node of that kind and opens its settings. A route has Start, Sequence, Auto decision, Sub flow and End; a plan has Start, Wait, Auto and Manual decision, Input screen, Sub flow and End; an input flow has Start, Ask, Fill, Auto decision, Run and End. Start is offered until the template has one. An installed suite may add kinds of its own, named with the suite.
Tidy
Lays the nodes out, left to right from the start, branches below.
The canvas
Drag a node to move it. Click a node or a wire to open its settings. Where a node sits is part of the design, compared and approved with the rest.
Wires
Where a run goes next. Draw one with Wire from here… in a node's settings, then click the node it goes to. A wire's words: its choice, if … (a condition), otherwise, or retry (dashed, a wait's way back). A wire from a manual decision starts as Choice 1, 2…
Drawing a wire
While a wire is being drawn, the line above the canvas names the node it starts from: click the node it goes to.
Problems
A node or wire outlined in red has something wrong; its settings say what.
− Fit +
Zoom out and in; the middle button shows the zoom and fits the drawing to the panel again. Ctrl or ⌘ and the wheel (or a pinch) zoom about the pointer; zoomed in, drag the empty ground to move about.
On a phone
The canvas is a list: each node with where it goes. Tap one for its settings.

The nodes

Start
Where every run begins: one per template, with the condition that starts it.
Sequence
A step of a route. Entering it sets the traveler's step and, if you say so, its state. It offers its transactions on the resources it allows; one that takes it on (or any change of the traveler's step) moves it along.
Auto decision
Decides at once from the run's context: its wires' conditions in order, the last may be otherwise.
Manual decision
A person picks one of its wires; each wire's label is the choice they see.
Wait
Shows a message and the time remaining. It goes on by itself, waits to be acknowledged, or also offers Retry.
Input screen
Collects into the context: list choices, typed values, files, images, links, and choices from a named query.
Sub flow
Runs another template of the same kind, passing values in and taking some back.
Ask
In an input flow: puts the cursor on one input of the form and says when to move on.
Fill
In an input flow: fills an input by itself, from another input, a lookup or a value.
Run
In an input flow: checks the form and confirms it.
End
Closes the run, with its outcome; in an input flow, starts again for the next one or stops.
A suite's node, suite missing
A node whose suite is not installed stays, and a run that reaches it stops there.

A node's settings

The drawer
Opens over the canvas when a node or wire is selected; × closes it. Its heading gives the node's label, kind and id.
Label
The name shown on the node and to people.
Checkboxes
Tick each that applies: the transactions done at a step, those that take the traveler on, the departments and groups who act.
Drop-downs
Pick one: the state a step marks, its screen, the template a sub flow runs, a script to run.
Values written as data
Settings, resources, what a sub flow passes and takes back, a query's parameters (and an input flow's conditions) are written as JSON, such as {"context": "lot.product"}; outlined red until it reads correctly.
Goes on to
The wires leaving this node; click one to open its settings.
Wire from here…
Starts a wire from this node: then click the node it goes to. An End has none.
Remove the node
Takes the node out, with every wire to and from it, after you confirm.
Its problems
What is wrong with this node, in words, under its settings.

Conditions

A condition
A start's, a wire's or the template's early end, built from what the run reads: its context, its values and the fields of its records. The box shows it in words; empty, it shows what none means (always, otherwise, never).
Edit (or Add a condition)
Click the box or Edit to open it as rows: a field, a comparison and a value or another field. Done closes it.
A row
Pick what it reads, how it compares, and the value; × removes the row.
All of these, any of these, none of these
A group of rows; its drop-down says how they combine. × removes the group.
+ condition, + group, + from a pattern…
Adds a row, a group, or a common condition (is in state, is one of, is empty, is between…) filled in with what this place reads.
JSON
The same condition as JSON, applied as soon as it reads; empty means none.
Try
Type a value for each thing it reads and see whether it holds, and why.
Its problems
What does not fit: JSON that does not read, or something this place cannot read (a field the run does not have).

Each kind's settings

Start: For travelers where, For events where
A route takes up a traveler made where this holds (at its step, if it is part-way); a plan's runs start where it holds. Empty: always.
Start: Due on
A plan: a date field of its subject. When that date arrives, the plan sets off by itself, where the condition also holds.
Start: sets off again
A plan: sets off again for a record once its last run there has ended.
Sequence: Done here
The transactions offered on a traveler at this step; elsewhere they are refused.
Sequence: …taking it on
Once one of these has run, the traveler goes on by this node's wire.
Sequence: Marks its state
The state the traveler is put in on entering, or left as it is.
Sequence: On resources where
The fields a resource must have to work it here, such as {"process": ["die_saw"]}; another is refused. Empty: any.
Sequence: Settings
Values for this step, such as limits, read by its transactions as {"node": "lsl"}.
Sequence: Its screen
The screen opened for this step, or none.
Sequence: In the guide at
Where this step is in the route's guide: a page of a PDF (3) or a moment of a video (0:45). A screen's file block that follows this route shows the step there, ticked once the lot has gone on.
Manual decision: Asks
The question the person answers by picking one of its wires.
Who
On a manual decision, an acknowledged wait and an input screen: tick the departments and groups who act on it.
Wait: Shows
The message shown while it waits.
Wait: For (seconds)
How long it waits; the time remaining is shown.
Wait: When the time is up
It goes on by itself, waits for someone to acknowledge, or waits with a Retry that goes back. For Retry, wire its way back and tick that wire as the retry wire.
Sub flow: Runs
The template it runs, of the same kind: a route runs another route for the same traveler, which goes through its steps and comes back; a plan waits for the other plan to end.
Sub flow: Passes
Values into its context: { name: expression }.
Sub flow: Takes back
Values from its context when it ends: { name here: its name there }.
End: Outcome
The word the run ends with, such as shipped or scrapped.

An input screen's fields

Asks
What the person is asked to do, shown above the fields.
Collects
One row per field, in the order the screen shows them. What the person enters is kept in the context under each field's name.
Name
Its name in the context, read by the decisions after as {"context": "name"}.
Label
What the person sees beside the field.
Type
Text, a whole or decimal number, yes or no, a list of choices, a file, an image, a link, or from a query.
Choices
For a list: type a choice and press Enter or pick Add "…"; × removes one.
required
Ticked, the screen cannot be sent without it.
up
Moves the field one place earlier on the screen.
+ a field
Adds a text field at the end; rename it and pick its type.

A field from a query

Its list from
A named query (Designer, Queries). Each person sees the rows it gives them.
Value (column)
The column whose value goes into the context; id when empty.
Shown (columns)
The columns the person sees, joined in this order. Type a column and press Enter.
Between them
What separates the shown columns, · when empty.
The query's parameters
Each parameter it declares (* if required), from the run's context ({"context": "lot.product"}), the person ({"user": "id"}) or a value.

An input flow's settings

Ask: Asks for
A transaction's input by name. On a screen: param (what it is opened with) or transaction.input. Suggestions appear as you type.
Ask: Prompt
Shown above the form while it asks; its label otherwise.
Ask: Moves on by
Enter (a scanner's too), Tab, either, a key of its own, or by itself once complete. A scan always finds its record first.
Ask: The key
Press it in the box, or type it: F1 to F12, or one character that is not a letter or a digit (a scanner's suffix).
Ask: Complete at (characters)
A label of fixed length moves on as soon as it is read whole.
Ask: …or when it matches
A regular expression the whole entry matches.
Ask: passed over when already filled
Ticked, it is skipped when the screen, a row or a fill has filled it already.
Ask: On an error
While its field shows an error or is required and empty: the cursor stays, or it goes on regardless.
Fill: Fills
The input it fills, named as an ask names one.
Fill: With
An expression: {"input": "lot"}, {"lookup": "lot.qty"}, {"param": "equipment"}, or a value.
Run: Runs, Confirming
On a screen with several transactions, which one it runs; and whether it waits for Enter on Confirm or confirms at once. A signature is always asked for.
End: Then
The start again, for the next one, or stop here.

A node's scripts

Scripts
A rule script run on entering and on leaving the node: the context in and out. A variable it sets is kept on the run, for the decisions and lists after; what it asks to write goes through the record's own rules, as the template. Not in an input flow.
On entering, On leaving
Pick a script, or none.
New script…, Edit
New script asks its name and puts it in this change, with a first test case. Edit opens the script under the canvas; Close hides it.
The script under the canvas
Its code, whether it compiles, a dry run that changes nothing, and its test cases, which the fitness test runs before the change is submitted.

A wire's settings

From → to
The two nodes it joins.
Taken when
From an auto decision: its wires are tried in order, and the first that holds is taken. Leave the last empty for otherwise.
Try earlier
From an auto decision: moves this wire one place up, to be tried sooner.
The choice
From a manual decision: what the person sees and picks.
the way back, behind its Retry button
From a wait that retries: tick it on the wire that goes back.
Remove the wire
Takes the wire out, after you confirm.

General

Label
The template's name, as people see it.
Kind
A route, a plan (OCAP) or an input flow. It decides the palette and the tabs. A template nobody has drawn on yet, made an input flow, starts with an ask, a run and an end, ready to change.
Description
What it is for, in a few words.
Runs
A route: on its own, taking up the travelers its start names, or only inside another route as its sub flow (a rework loop, a test segment drawn once). A sub route needs no scenario of its own.
Ends early when
Checked after each write to the traveler, such as when it is merged or scrapped: the run ends there. Empty: never.

Context

Context
What every run carries, read by its decisions as {"context": "name"}.
The list
Its records (from Participants), the values it starts with, and what its input screens collect and its sub flows return.
A value's name
Rename it here; a name already in use is not taken.
Its value
What a run starts with: a number, true or false, or text. Its scripts may change it.
+ a value
Adds a value, starting at 0.

Participants

Participants
The records the template works with, in its context by these names: the traveler going through it, the resources it is worked on, its subject, what it reads.
Object
Only objects whose design says they take part in flows (on their General tab) are offered.
Takes part as
Traveler, resource, subject or reference, among those its object allows.
From
Its own record, or the one another participant's field points to.
+ a record
Adds a participant.
What the template itself may do
The roles it acts with, per object, when it marks a step or a state and when its scripts write. Give it the least it needs.

Scenarios

Scenarios
Its evidence: a record walked through it in a sandbox, each step expecting the node reached. The fitness test runs them on every submit; a new or changed template without one, or with one that fails, is not submitted. A plan set off only as a sub flow needs none.
Make one in the sandbox
Opens the sandbox on this template, to walk a record through it and save the scenario.
A scenario
Its name, passed or failed at the last fitness test, its records, and its steps with what each expects. A failure says why.
open in the sandbox
Opens it in the sandbox to run or change it.
remove
Takes it out of the template, after you confirm.

Stewards

Stewards
Tick the departments that approve a change to this template. The stewards of the transactions it offers and of the objects it may write approve it too.

JSON

The template as JSON
All of it as data, for integrators.
Apply JSON
Replaces the draft with what you wrote (its name must stay); every check runs on it. If it does not read, an error says why and the draft stays as it was.
