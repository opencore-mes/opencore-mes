A flow template is drawn here: a route a lot goes along step by step, or a plan (an OCAP) an event sets off. It is approved like every other design.

Its tabs

The tabs
Flow is the canvas; General, its kind and when a run ends early; Context, the values every run carries; Participants, its records and what the template itself may do; Stewards, who approves it; Scenarios, its evidence; Changes, what differs from the live version; JSON, all of it as data.
Scenarios
A record walked through it in a sandbox, each step expecting the node reached: made in the sandbox, run again by the fitness test on every submit.
Copilot
Ask the AI to draft, check or explain the template. It works as you, and never submits.

Drawing

Add (the palette)
Adds a node of that kind. A route has Start, Sequence, Auto decision and End; a plan has Start, Wait, Auto and Manual decision, Input screen, Sub flow and End.
Tidy
Lays the nodes out, left to right from the start, branches below.
The canvas
Drag a node to move it. Click a node or a wire to open its settings. Where a node sits is part of the design, compared and approved with the rest.
Wires
Where a run goes next. Draw one with Wire from here… in a node's settings, then click the node it goes to. A wire's words: its choice, if … (a condition), otherwise, or retry (dashed, a wait's way back).
Problems
A node or wire outlined in red has something wrong; its settings say what.

The nodes

Start
Where every run begins: one per template, with the condition that starts it.
Sequence
A step of a route. Entering it sets the lot's step and, if you say so, its state. It offers its transactions on the resources it allows; Move out (or any change of the lot's step) takes it on.
Auto decision
Decides at once from the run's context: its wires' conditions in order, the last may be otherwise.
Manual decision
A person picks one of its wires; each wire's label is the choice they see.
Wait
Shows a message and the time remaining. It goes on by itself, waits to be acknowledged, or also offers Retry.
Input screen
Collects into the context: list choices, typed values, files, images and links.
Sub flow
Runs another template, passing values in and taking some back.
End
Closes the run, with its outcome.

A node's settings

The drawer
Opens over the canvas when a node or wire is selected; × closes it.
Checkboxes
Tick each that applies: the transactions done at a step, those that take the lot on, who acts.
Drop-downs
Pick one: the state a step marks, its screen, a script to run.
Conditions and settings
Written as data, such as {"gt": [{"context": "lot.qty"}, 5]}; outlined red until it reads correctly.
Scripts
A rule script run on entering and on leaving the node: the context in and out. What it asks to write goes through the record's own rules.
