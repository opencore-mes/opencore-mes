The plant's organization as one draft: its departments and who approves for each, its people, who holds each role, how changes are approved, and the plant's formats, look, retention and sign-in. You change it in a change request like any other design; governance and the departments concerned approve it, and nothing changes for anyone until it executes. Outside a change, the People & departments page shows it read only, with Change people & departments (or Open the change in progress) to start one.

Everywhere in it

The tabs
Changes, Departments, People, Roles, Approvals, Formats, Theme, Retention, Integrity, Sign-in and JSON. Click one to open it in this window.
… more
When the tabs do not all fit, the last ones are under this button: click it and pick one.
The number on a tab
How many things on that tab differ from the live organization. The number on Changes counts them all.
Marked changes
A department, a person or an object's roles that this change adds or alters is marked, so you see what you touched.
This window shows
When the change holds more than People & departments, pick here what this window shows.
Undo, Redo
Take back the last edit, or bring it again (⌘Z or Ctrl+Z, ⇧⌘Z or Ctrl+Y, outside a text box). Any edit on any tab is one step.
Lists of people and groups
The chosen ones show as chips; × on a chip removes it. Type to find more, then press Enter or click one to add it; Backspace in the empty box removes the last. Past 30, the list shows a count: type to find one.
Remove
Asks you to confirm first. What you remove leaves only this change; Undo brings it back.

Changes

The summary
How many differences from the live organization: changed, added, removed. Reviewers and approvers read this first.
By tab
The differences grouped by the tab they are on, with how many.
Open Departments, Open People…
Opens that tab in this window, to see or change it there.
Each difference
Click it to fold or unfold it: lines taken out are marked −, lines added +.

Departments

What this changes for people
Shown when the draft moves people's roles: someone who leaves a department loses its roles, and joining one gives its roles. People new in this change are only counted.
Keep them
Gives the roles a person would lose to that person directly, so the move takes nothing away.
Governance
The department that approves what nobody else stewards, new departments and new people. Pick it in the drop-down.
A department
One box per department: its name, with its id beside it. A department is approved by its own approvers and by governance when it changes.
Name
What everyone sees. The id beside the heading never changes.
Members
Who belongs to it, and so holds the roles given to it. Type a name or a sign-in id to add someone. Someone you take out leaves its approval steps too.
Approval, in order
The steps of the department's approval, signed one after the other. Each step is signed by one of its approvers: never the change's author or reviewer, and never whoever signed the step before.
A step's name
What the step is called, e.g. Manager or Quality lead.
Its approvers
Chosen among the department's members only. With no members yet, it says to add members first.
Earlier (the arrow)
Moves the step one place up, to be signed sooner.
remove
Takes the step out, after you confirm. Shown only while the department has two steps or more.
+ step
Adds a step at the end, with no approvers yet.
Add a department
Type its id (lower case, starting with a letter, e.g. it) and its name, then press Add. It starts with one step, Approver: add its members and approvers.

People

Add a person
Type their sign-in id as your directory knows it (lower case letters, digits, - and _, e.g. mark or 104523) and their name, then press Add. Put them in a department on the Departments tab.
Import
Choose a CSV (as Export writes it, or saved from Excel) with the columns id, name, active, departments (department ids, separated by spaces or commas). You see what it would do and confirm with Put them in this change. New people are added, the others take what their row says; people not in the file stay as they are.
Export
Downloads everyone in this draft as people-<date>.csv, which Excel opens. Edit it, then Import it back.
After an import
Says what went into the change and how many rows were not taken, or why nothing was (every row already as it says, an unknown department). dismiss hides it.
What this changes for people
The roles people lose or gain through the departments they move between, as on the Departments tab, with Keep them.
Find people
Type at least two letters: the start of a name or a department, or any part of a sign-in id. With nothing typed, the list shows only the people this change adds or changes.
The list
Each person found: Id, Name, Active, Departments. It draws more rows as you scroll; the count is below it.
Name
Change it in place.
Active
Untick it when someone leaves: once the change executes, they can no longer sign in. People are never deleted, because the audit trail names them.
Departments
Where they belong, read only here: change it with a department's Members, or by Import.

Roles

By object, By group or person
Two ways to the same roles: each object with who holds its roles, or one department, group or person with everything they hold. A change to an object's roles is approved by that object's stewards.
Find an object
Type part of an object's name, a role, or who holds one. Objects this change touches come first.
An object
Its roles and their holders on one line; click it to unfold. Besides the designed objects, Designer, Queries, Sign-in, Privacy and Data integrity hold the platform's own roles (designer, reviewer, analyst, administrator, officer).
A role
Who holds it: departments, groups or people. Type to give it to another; × takes it away.
Show more
Thirty objects are drawn at a time; more come as you scroll to it, or when you press it.
Find a department, a group or a person
In By group or person: pick who, to see and change every role they hold.
… holds
The roles given to them directly. × (Take it away) removes one.
…and through their departments and groups
For a person: the roles that come with where they belong, and through which. Change those on the department or group.
Give another role
Type an object and a role (e.g. lot operator) and pick it to give it to them directly.

Certifications

The certifications the plant recognizes: ITAR, a cleanroom grade, a customer's clearance. An object may reserve records to one (its Roles & policies tab). Governance approves the list.

The list
Each certification's id (what designs name: itar), its name and what it is for, and the objects that require it. One an object requires says in use and cannot be removed.
Id, Name, Add
Type an id (lower case letters, digits and _) and a name, then Add.

Who holds one is kept as Certification records (Organization, in the navigator): the person, which certification, valid from and until, revoked or reinstated. It counts while it is active and in date.

Approvals

Standing approvers
For each kind of design (objects, rule scripts, services, connections, transactions, screens, report layouts, named queries, people & departments), tick the departments that also approve every change to it, whoever stewards it. They join the stewards; they do not replace them.
Reads every record
People, departments or groups who may read every object's records, those designed later included, except fields a policy hides. They write nothing through this; for IT or an auditor. Approved by governance.
Approval level
How much approval a change needs before it executes. Pick one; every change is still signed and kept in the audit trail.
Full
A reviewer who did not design it, then an approver of each department it touches, in their steps. Keep it if the plant answers to regulators (Part 11, Annex 11).
One approval
No review: the first signature by an approver of a department it touches (never its designer) executes it.
None
Its designer signs it and it executes: for a small plant with nobody else to ask.
Setup is open
While the plant is being set up, a designer's change executes on their own signature, each one marked in the audit trail. Untick it to end setup; it ends once someone besides the designers can review.
Emergency changes: Allowed
Ticked, a change that cannot wait may be submitted as an emergency, with a reason: one approver's signature makes it live, and it is reviewed afterwards.
Reviewed afterwards within (days)
How long the after-the-fact review of an emergency change may take: 1 to 30 days, 3 when empty.

Formats

Locale
How numbers are written (1,250.5 or 1.250,5). Type one, such as de-DE, or pick from the suggestions; empty means en-US.
Dates written
The order of day, month and year on every page and in forms. Stored dates stay YYYY-MM-DD.
Times
24-hour (14:05) or 12-hour (2:05 PM).
The week begins on
The first day of the week in calendars.
The plant's clock (time zone)
Times are shown on this clock, wherever a person is. Type or pick a zone such as Europe/Paris; empty means the server's.
Reads as
Now, a number and a date written the way you set them. Pages follow when next opened, once the change executes.

Theme

Light or dark
Each person chooses, or always light, or always dark for everyone (a shop floor's screens, a control room).
Name in the top bar
Up to 40 characters; empty shows OpenCore MES.
Label beside it
The site, plant or line, e.g. Plant 1 · Lyon.
Light, Dark
The colours for each scheme: Accent (links, buttons, the current step), OK, Warn and Danger, which draw the states' tones.
A colour
Pick it with the swatch, or type it as #rrggbb. Empty the text box to go back to the default.
The preview
A top bar, a button, state badges and a link, drawn with your colours.
Contrast
Each colour must read at 4.5:1 or better against its background; one that does not is named with its ratio.

Retention

Kind
Each kind of data the plant keeps, with what it covers.
Kept for
Type days (365), years (2 years) or forever; empty keeps the default. Below it: the default and the least it may be.
Past it
removed: the platform deletes what is older, every few hours. counted, kept: archived records and the audit trail are never deleted; the Data retention page counts what is past its period.
Problems
A period that does not read, is below the least allowed or above the most, or an audit trail kept for less time than the records it describes, is named here.

Integrity

Report object
Where a closed data integrity finding raises its non-conformance report: one of the plant's objects, through its own policies and rules. None keeps the reports on the Data integrity page and in the audit trail.
Part of the report
For each part (what happened, why, the decision, the action taken, the finding), pick the object's field that takes it, or Not kept. A field takes one part only.
Required fields: always
A required field no part fills gets the same value every time, such as a severity or a category: pick it or type it.
Problems
What is missing or wrong in this setting. Who reviews findings is a role: Roles, Data integrity.

Sign-in

The id is called
What the sign-in page calls the id people type: Windows user name, Employee number, Badge number. Empty shows Username.
Shown in the empty box
A hint in the empty id box, such as PLANT\username or 6 digits.
The plant's domains
Type a domain (PLANT, plant.local) and press Enter or pick Add "…"; commas add several. Typed before a \ or / or after an @, a listed domain is dropped: PLANT\jdoe signs in as jdoe. × removes one.
The sample
The id box as the sign-in page will show it, and an example of a domain dropped.
Problems
A label too long or a domain that does not read is named here.

JSON

The organization as JSON
All of it as data. Edit it here for a large change at once.
Apply JSON
Replaces the draft with what you wrote; every check runs on it as on any other edit. If it does not read, an error says why and the draft stays as it was.
