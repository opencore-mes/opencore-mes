A design element is a design of a kind a suite adds to the platform. What every element has (its name, label, description and stewards) is edited here; what its kind adds is edited on Design, by the suite's own editor. It is reviewed and approved like every other design. If its suite is taken out, the element stays as it is, labelled, and works again once the suite is back. On a live design, or a change you may not edit, every control here is read only.

Its tabs

The tabs
General opens first. Click a tab to see its settings; the open one is highlighted.
A number on a tab
How many things on that tab differ from the live version. On Changes, the total.
Undo
Nothing live changes until the change executes; Undo (⌘Z or Ctrl+Z) takes back your last edit.

Copilot

The conversation
Ask the AI to draft, check or explain the element. It works as you, saves into this draft, and never submits. The conversation is kept with the change: it picks up here any time.
Ask the copilot…
Type what you want. ⌘ Enter or Ctrl+Enter sends; you can paste or drop a file into the box.
Attach
Adds a picture, a PDF, a CSV file or an Excel workbook (up to 5); × on a file takes it off.
Send
Saves your unsaved edits first, then sends. Greyed out while it works, or when no AI is set up.
New conversation
Asks first, then deletes the conversation kept with this change. The draft stays as it is.

Changes

The differences
What this change does to the element against the live version, grouped by tab, each of its kind's settings on its own: how many changed, were added and removed. Reviewers and approvers see the same.
Open …
Jumps to the tab where that group is edited.
A difference
Marked new, changed or removed; click it to open or close it. Lines marked − are the live version's, lines marked + the draft's.

General

Name
Fixed once created.
Kind
Fixed: the kind of design it is, and the suite that adds it. If that suite is not installed here, it says so.
Label
A text field: what people know it by.
Description
A text box: what it is for.

Design

Its kind's settings
Drawn by the suite that adds this kind: its controls, and its checks, are the suite's own. Its problems are listed with the change's others before it can be submitted.
As JSON
When the suite brings no editor for this kind, or is not installed here, the element is edited as JSON instead, applied with Apply JSON. While its suite is away it stays as it is.

Stewards

Departments
Tick each department that approves every change to it.

JSON

The element as JSON
All of it as data, its kind's settings included. Edit it here, then apply it.
Apply JSON
Replaces the draft with it; every check runs as on any other edit, its suite's included. Its name must stay the same; what is wrong is said in red beside the change.
