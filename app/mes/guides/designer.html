Every change reaches the plant the same way: designed here, reviewed, approved by everyone it concerns, then executed. A live design is shown read only until you start a change.

The change

Its title and state
What this change is called, and where it stands.
The four steps
Design, Review, Approval, Execution: the one lit is where it is now.
Change it, Designer
On a live design: start a change of it, or go back to the designer's home.

Its windows

This window shows
A drop-down of the change's parts (objects, transactions, screens, flow templates): pick the one to work on.
Undo, Redo
Takes back the last edit to the change, in any of its windows, and puts it forward again. ⌘Z or Ctrl+Z, and ⇧⌘Z or Ctrl+Y, do the same outside a text box. Typing in one box is one step; an undo is saved like any edit. The steps start again when someone else's edit to the change comes in.
Change also
Brings a live design into this change (an object, a transaction, a screen…): pick it, then Bring in. It is then edited here and approved with the rest.
+ service, + flow template…
Adds a new part to the change: choose its kind, type its name, then Add.
Split
Opens another window beside this one (up to three), to compare or copy.
Maximize
Fills the browser window with the designer; Restore brings the navigator back.
Its tabs
Each part's settings, by topic. A number on a tab counts what differs from the live version.

In every editor

Lists of words (chips)
Roles, values, states, fields, columns, people: each is a chip, and × removes it. Click into the box to see what exists, type to narrow it, Enter or a click adds. Where a new one is allowed, Add “…” is offered (several at once with commas); where it must exist already, a word that is not one is said not to match. Backspace in the empty box removes the last.
Conditions (Only when…)
The condition as a rule, in a box: click it, or Edit, to build it from fields, the person and values; JSON shows it as data; Try tests it on values you type. A dashed box means none (it always holds, or as it says).
Remove
Asks first, naming what goes. It is taken out of this change only; Undo brings it back.
changed, new
Marks what differs from the live version; a number on a tab counts its differences, and Changes lists them all.

Try it in a sandbox
Runs the draft on copies of real records, in a database of its own: nothing real changes.

The checks, every script's test cases and the transactions' scenarios. Submitting runs it again.
Will need approval from
The departments that approve it, and because of what.
Submit for review
Sends it to its reviewer. Until then, only you and your co-designers see it.
