The problem

Every new field, rule and screen waits on a vendor.

The people who know the process cannot change the system that runs it. OpenCore MES lets them.

Solution

Everything is a design.

Designed in the browser, never written into the product.

  • Objects. Lots, machines, work orders: fields, states, roles.
  • Screens and transactions. Station screens, move-in, track-out, built from blocks.
  • Conditions in words. Built from the fields, read back in plain words; no code.
  • Copy and change. Any design is the start of the next.
  • Named queries. The queries the plant relies on, approved like the rest.
  • An AI that drafts. Describe it; it drafts the change. AI
