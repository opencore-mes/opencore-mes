The problem

Changes reach the plant untested, unapproved and untraced.

A hotfix on Friday, a field edited in the database, an auditor asking who and why. OpenCore MES answers each.

Solution

One path for every change.

A field, a rule, a screen, an approver: all go the same way, on the record.

  1. Design

    Drafted alone, with co-designers or the AI, with why.

  2. Review

    Someone who did not design it checks the frozen draft.

  3. Approval

    Each department it touches signs, never its authors.

  4. Execution

    Live exactly as approved, everywhere, no restart.

Solution

Made for plants that answer to auditors.

Part 11 practices built in; SOC 2, ISO 27001 and HIPAA mapped control by control in the repository.

  • Your sign-in. Single sign-on, LDAP or Active Directory, or passwords with a second factor.
  • An audit trail that checks itself. Append only, chained, verified every 15 minutes.
  • Segregation of duties. Nobody approves what they designed or reviewed.
  • Signed by two. An operator and a verifier, each proving it is them.
  • Record changes that wait. Edits your design names go to the people responsible.
  • Emergency changes. Live on one signature, reviewed within days.
  • Sensitive fields. Hidden by default, shown with a reason, never sent to the AI.
  • Retention and erasure. Periods approved like any change; the audit trail kept.
  • Backups you have restored. Nightly, encrypted, a restore tested every month.
  • "Why can't I?" Every refusal explained from the rule that decided it.
