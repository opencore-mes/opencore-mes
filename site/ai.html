The problem

An answer about the floor takes a week.

A report request, a queue, a spreadsheet out of date by the time it arrives. And AI you cannot let near production data.

Solution

Ask the plant. Get a report.

Plain words in; a page of sentences, figures, charts and tables out, from live records.

  • With the asker's rightsIt reads only what they may read; every query audited.
  • It reads, never changesAdvice only; acting is a person's transaction.
  • AI assisted linesGive a line its shift goal; it says which lots first, and why.
  • Every morning by itselfKeep a prompt; have it asked on a schedule.
  • Layouts your engineers approveThe blocks and their order, designed like any screen.
  • Photos, specs, spreadsheetsAttach them; it compares them with the line.

Solution

AI drafts. People decide.

Describe what the plant needs; the AI drafts it as a change for people to approve.

  • Drafts, checks, testsFields, rules with their tests, screens, routes.
  • Marked on the recordReviewers see what the AI wrote.
  • Never the one who signsIt never reviews, approves or makes anything live.
  • Claude, or your own modelOr any model your company runs itself.
